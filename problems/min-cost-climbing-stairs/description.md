You are given an array `cost` where `cost[i]` is the price of leaving step `i`. Once you pay it you may advance one or two steps. You may start on step `0` or step `1` for free, and you finish when you move beyond the last step. Return the minimum total price needed to reach that top.

Constraints:
- `2 <= len(cost) <= 1000`
- `0 <= cost[i] <= 999`
