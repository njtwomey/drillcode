Given an `m x n` grid `heights` of cell elevations, the Pacific touches the top and left edges and the Atlantic touches the bottom and right edges. Water moves between 4-directionally adjacent cells whenever the destination is at the same or lower height. Return all `[row, col]` coordinates (0-indexed) from which water can reach both oceans, in any order.

Constraints:

- Grid is up to 200 x 200
- Heights are non-negative integers
