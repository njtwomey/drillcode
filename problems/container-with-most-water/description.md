`height` gives the heights of vertical lines at x = 0, 1, 2, and so on. Pick two lines that, together with the x-axis, form a container, and return the largest area of water it can hold. The container cannot be tilted, and the water level is limited by the shorter of the two lines.

Constraints:
- `2 <= len(height) <= 10^5`
- `0 <= height[i] <= 10^4`
