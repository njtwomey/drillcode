Given two strings `s` and `t` of equal length, return `true` if `s` can be turned into `t` by replacing characters consistently. Every occurrence of a character in `s` must be replaced by the same character, and no two different characters of `s` may be replaced by the same character. A character may map to itself.

Constraints:
- `1 <= len(s) <= 5 * 10^4`
- `len(t) == len(s)`
- Both strings consist of valid ASCII characters
