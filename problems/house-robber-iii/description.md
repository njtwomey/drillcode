The houses form a binary tree: `root` is the entrance and each house is linked to its parent. A thief robs houses holding `val` money each, but robbing two directly linked houses (a parent and one of its children) triggers an alarm. Return the maximum total the thief can take without ever robbing two linked houses.

Constraints:

- Between 1 and 10000 nodes
- Node values lie in [0, 10^4]
